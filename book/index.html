BOOKING QUESTIONS

Simple answers about direct booking and custom sacred arrangements.

Is payment collected on this website?

No. This page sends an enquiry. A booking is confirmed only after availability, inclusions and the written quotation are accepted.

Can you plan a custom pilgrimage?

Yes. Request a custom route, family or group journey, elderly-friendly pace, hotel coordination or a multi-city pilgrimage.

Can I request pooja, anushthan or ceremony coordination?

Yes. You can request pooja, anushthan, shraddha or pind daan assistance, wedding or sacred ceremony coordination. Availability and exact inclusions are confirmed separately.

How do I discuss special requirements?

Send the form or contact Punya Yatra directly on WhatsApp at +91 93358 74326. Mention mobility needs, preferred temples, arrival point and any time-sensitive ritual requirements.
